Research

Device-to-system co-design for next-generation computing.

Google Scholar Selected Publications

My research explores the co-design of emerging memory, AI hardware, three-dimensional integration, advanced packaging, and multiphysics systems. I am interested in connecting device-level innovations with circuits, architectures, packaging technologies, and system-level performance.

Research Areas

AI Hardware and Architecture

Energy-efficient accelerator architectures and memory systems for artificial intelligence, large language models, and data-intensive computing.

Emerging Memory

Device-to-system evaluation of emerging nonvolatile memories, including their latency, energy, density, reliability, and architectural implications.

3D Integration

Monolithic and heterogeneous three-dimensional integration for improving memory bandwidth, communication efficiency, and computing density.

Advanced Packaging

Chiplets, glass substrates, three-dimensional memory stacking, power delivery, and thermal-aware package design.

Multiphysics Co-Design

Integrated thermal, electrical, mechanical, signal-integrity, and power-integrity analysis for advanced computing systems.

Design Technology Co-Optimization

Cross-layer optimization connecting semiconductor devices, interconnects, circuits, architectures, and physical systems.

Selected Research Projects

3D Heterogeneous Integration for AI Accelerators

This research investigates three-dimensional integration strategies for improving memory bandwidth, data-movement efficiency, hardware utilization, and system-level energy efficiency in AI accelerators.

Topics: AI accelerators, large language models, heterogeneous integration, high-bandwidth memory, and architecture–package co-design.

Emerging Memory for AI Inference

This work evaluates emerging memory technologies as dense and energy-efficient storage solutions for AI inference systems. The research connects device characteristics with array-level latency, energy, architecture, and system performance.

Topics: Emerging nonvolatile memory, FeFET, memory arrays, circuit modeling, and AI inference.

Advanced Packaging and Multiphysics Analysis

This research studies thermal management, power delivery, signal integrity, mechanical reliability, and system integration in advanced three-dimensional semiconductor packages.

Topics: Advanced packaging, thermal analysis, PDN, SI/PI, reliability, glass substrates, and three-dimensional stacking.

Monolithic 3D Integration

This research investigates fabrication, thermal constraints, interconnect technologies, and circuit applications for monolithic three-dimensional integrated systems.

Topics: Monolithic 3D IC, sequential integration, BEOL devices, thermal processing, and voltage-regulator applications.
